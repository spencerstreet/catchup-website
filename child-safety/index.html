Child safety standards.

Catchup has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page sets out our commitment, how to report a concern, and how we respond — in line with the standards expected of apps on the App Store and Google Play.

Section 01

Our commitment.

Catchup is built for organising real plans with people you already know. We do not permit child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, or any content or behaviour that endangers children — in events, comments, messages, photos, profiles, or group chats.

Any account used to exploit or endanger a child is removed on sight.

We remove the account permanently and report it to the relevant authorities. This applies without exception.

Section 02

Who can use Catchup.

Catchup is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at, marketed to, or designed for children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 16 to hold an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we remove it. See the Children section of our Privacy Policy for more.

Section 03

Reporting a concern.

Every event, comment, message, photo, and profile in Catchup can be reported directly from the app: open the ⋯ menu on the item, choose Report, and pick a reason. Reports about a child's safety are treated as our highest priority.

You can also write to us directly at support@keentocatchup.com — put “Child safety” in the subject line so it reaches us immediately.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.

Section 04

How we respond.

We review every report. Where we find child sexual abuse material or exploitation, or a credible risk to a child's safety, we remove the content, permanently remove the account, and refer the matter to the relevant law enforcement or child protection authority. We preserve records relevant to a report, consistent with our Privacy Policy and applicable law, so they can be provided to investigators.

Section 05

Point of contact.

Trust & Safety at Spencer Street Limited is responsible for responding to child safety reports and for keeping this page current.

support@keentocatchup.com
Section 06

Working with authorities.

We cooperate with law enforcement and child protection agencies investigating child sexual abuse and exploitation, and comply with applicable mandatory reporting obligations in the jurisdictions where Catchup operates.
